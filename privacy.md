# Privacy Policy — LoL News Hunter

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

LoL News Hunter is an unofficial, fan-made Telegram bot designed to deliver League of Legends news and updates.

**Data Processing**

The bot uses a configured Telegram chat ID to deliver news. Its current scheduled news-delivery functionality does not require users to submit personal information.

**Data Storage**

The bot stores identifiers of previously processed news links to help prevent duplicate notifications. Telegram and GitHub may process technical information according to their respective privacy policies.

**Third-Party Sources**

News articles, images, and links may originate from external websites. These websites have their own privacy practices.

**Contact**

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the bot administrator through the contact method published alongside this policy.

**Disclaimer**

LoL News Hunter is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Riot Games.
